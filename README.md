# Gym Membership & Retention Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes gym membership behavior, customer retention, revenue performance, and service engagement using Power BI.

The dashboard helps identify:
- Membership trends
- Customer engagement patterns
- Retention performance
- Revenue opportunities
- Trainer and service utilization insights

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

---

## 📈 KPIs Included
- Total Members
- Active Members
- Total Revenue
- Retention Rate
- Average Gym Duration

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Premium members generate high-value revenue opportunities
- Retention is strongly connected to member engagement
- Service usage patterns impact customer activity
- Personal training and premium services increase retention potential
- Certain trainers show stronger engagement performance

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Launch upselling campaigns for standard members
- Improve retention through engagement programs
- Optimize trainer scheduling during peak hours
- Bundle premium services to increase revenue
- Target inactive members with re-engagement campaigns
